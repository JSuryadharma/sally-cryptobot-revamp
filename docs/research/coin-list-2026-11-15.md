# Coin list refresh (2026-11-15)

Run on Joe's Mac with `scripts/coinListResearch.mjs` (branch claude/project-thread-lh4b9n). Window 2023-12-01 to 2026-11-15. Period A = first 24 months (used to pick coins), period B = last 12 months (the check). Cost 0.2% + 0.05% slippage per fill.

## How coins were picked (decided on period A only)
1. Among Binance's 50 most-traded USDT pairs plus the old list. Stablecoins, wrapped coins, tokenized stocks and the 3 banned coins (LSK, VTHO, ASTR) left out.
2. Liquid: median daily volume at least $10M (30-day or 1-year). The engine also needs $20M on the breakout day itself.
3. At least about 18 months of history and price data up to yesterday.
4. The robot's two breakout rules (55-day +3R, 20-day +1R, 3-ATR stop, BTC above 200-day) made at least +3R combined in period A, each coin traded alone.

## Honest read
- The big 3-year gain is mostly in period A, where the coins were chosen, so part of it is hindsight. On the fresh 12 months (B) the new list is about level with the old one (+22.6% vs +21.9%), with a slightly bigger drawdown. B has only 13 trades because BTC was under its 200-day for most of it.
- Adding one coin (INJ) moved the 3-year result from +147% to +120%. Results are sensitive to which coins grab the 6 slots on busy days, so treat the gap as rough.
- Within the new list, TRX, ADA and PEPE lost money in the full-engine run even though they passed the screen. Worth rechecking in 3 months.
- Clear wins regardless: drops stale TON and too-thin ATOM, and a fresh database no longer starts on just 5 coins (+77% vs +147%).
- Script oddity: the "0.3% cost" column sometimes shows more profit than 0.2%. Likely path dependence (different sizing changes which trades get the 6 slots), not checked further.
